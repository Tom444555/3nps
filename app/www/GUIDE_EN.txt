LOOPSMITHS / 3NPS LOOPER – USER GUIDE (English)
================================================

Switch the language with the small “EN / DE” button next to the app name at the top.
The app restarts briefly when you switch. Your recordings, songs and settings stay as they are.

The app runs completely on your device: no account, no server, no data collection.
Everything you record or write is stored only on this device (back it up, see section 11).


1. GETTING STARTED
------------------
- iPad: open the web page in Safari, tap Share → “Add to Home Screen”. Start the app from its icon.
  A new version becomes active when you open the app twice (the version is shown at the top, e.g. “v59”).
- Connect your audio interface or amp (e.g. Spark LIVE via USB) before opening the input.
- Tap “Open input” in the Looper. The meters next to the tracks now show your signal.
  Tap the button again (“Input open”) to close the input – the app then stops listening completely
  and does not reopen it by itself. REC or another tap on the button opens it again.
- iPadOS gives web apps only one input channel (mono). Stereo works in the Mac app via Chrome.


2. LOOPER
---------
- Three stereo tracks. Tap REC on track 1 and play your idea. Tap again: the loop plays.
  Tap again: overdub. Undo with ↶, delete with ✕ (tap twice).
- Loop length: free (the first recording sets the length and tempo) or a fixed number of bars.
- Count-in, “recording starts with the first note” (trigger), and “remove the gap at the start”
  are in the “Recording” card. New tracks are aligned to track 1 automatically (±60 ms).
- Load an audio file (MP3, WAV, M4A, AIFF, FLAC …) onto a track: key, tempo and bars are detected.
  Short, even files become a loop; longer songs keep the whole file and you choose a section.
- Above the tracks the “What fits” box shows the current and next chord, a matching scale with its
  notes, the pentatonic, and the next chord change (target note, leading tones). “Hold” freezes it.
- Drums and drone follow the loop. “Start all” starts tracks, drums and drone together.
- “Export for Logic” creates a ZIP with all tracks as 24-bit WAV (all starting on bar 1),
  a mix, and a MIDI file with tempo, key, chords as markers and the drum pattern. A READ ME explains
  the import step by step.
- “→ Song” takes the current loop as a part (verse, chorus …) into a song in Songwriting.


3. EDITOR (✂ on a track)
------------------------
- Waveform with bars, beats and 16ths. Tap the timeline to jump. Drag the orange handles to select.
- Cut: trim, halve, double. Edit (selection): cut out, copy, paste, duplicate, silence, reverse,
  fade in/out, ±3 dB, normalize. The loop length always stays the same. Undo/redo for every step.
- “⇔ Move loop”: drag the whole waveform; “Snap” locks the grabbed hit to bar, beat or 16th.
- Sync: shift by beats or 10 ms, fine align to the other tracks, first note on bar 1,
  fit to loop length (time-stretch, pitch unchanged), ½ or 2× tempo.
- “Check loop” tests whole bars, bar 1 at the start and a clean transition; “Fix it” corrects them.
- Full screen editor: the foot pedal controls fine steps (◀ 1 ms · 1 ms ▶ · switch handle · listen).


4. DRUMS, DRONE, EQ
-------------------
- More than 40 grooves (blues, rock, funk, latin, electronic …) with fills, swing and human feel.
  The designer edits every step: tap a cell for on → accent → ghost → off. Save your own patterns.
- “Room” is the drum reverb. Switching it off saves CPU if playback through a USB device stutters.
- The drone plays the root of the key (several sound worlds). It can follow the key of a track.
- 7-band EQ per track and for the drums, with presets.


5. FOOT PEDAL
-------------
- Any pedal that sends as a keyboard works (M-VAVE Chocolate, AirTurn, PageFlip, iRig BlueTurn …).
  Pair it in the iPad Bluetooth settings and set it to keyboard mode (M-VAVE: CubeSuite “Keyboard A” or “B”).
- “Last” shows which key arrives with every press. Other keys: “Learn custom mapping”.
- “Hold = undo” needs a pedal that stays pressed while held.


6. IMPROVISATION
----------------
- Follows the looper, the backing track or the song. The chord strip shows up to 6 bars and scrolls
  along; the next change is marked a little early so you can prepare.
- “Playing now”: chord name and degree, notes of the key, a small chord shape (tap to hear it),
  and the chord tones.
- “What fits over it”: modes, pentatonic, blues scale, target notes – each with notes and a short
  explanation of the sound. “To fretboard” shows it in Technique.
- “Next change”: common notes, leading tones and the target note (third of the next chord).
- “Listen” detects the notes you play through the input and counts chord tone / scale / clash.
- Idea dice: small exercises for your next solo. Start / pause / resume the loop from here.

- Lyrics (🎤): shows the lyrics of the playing song or loaded file in a lane under the chord strip,
  with the current word lit and the full line below. Paste lyrics for a file, then “● Tap timing”:
  play and tap the big button on every word. Fix words by tapping them (replace, split, join,
  ◀ ▶ by a 16th) or drag them in the lane. “↻” re-taps a single line. Times are stored in bars and
  beats, so they stay in sync. Lyrics of other artists: for your own practice only.
- Sing along tab: the lyrics big, every word fills while it is sung, the translation below and the next
  line in small print. Beat dots and a “3 – 2 – 1 – ♪” count-in before each entry after a pause.
  “↻ Practise line” loops the current line with a one-bar run-up, also at 75 % or 50 % without changing
  the pitch. “🎤 Check timing” detects your vocal entries and marks every word green (on time, ±70 ms),
  blue (early) or orange (late). Translation: one line per lyric line, typed in or made with Claude.
- “🤖 Detect lyrics automatically” (for loaded song files): choose the language, and the app transcribes the
  sung lyrics on the device (Whisper model, about 100 MB loaded once, then works offline), places every word
  on its beat and splits lines – then you only correct them. “Economy” uses a smaller model and less memory;
  if the iPad reloads the app during detection, it switches to Economy by itself.
- The lyrics lane under the chords is as big as the chord strip, with the same grid (bars, beats, 16ths) and
  play line. Drag a word to move it (a line shows the bar.beat it snaps to), tap a word to change it right there
  (empty or ✕ = delete, several words = split), tap an empty spot to insert a word.
- The first tap on a word (or an empty spot) widens that bar: chord and lyrics fill the whole strip and the view stays
  there until “✓ Done”. Drag words; tap again to change a word. “‹ Bar / Bar ›” moves to the previous/next bar,
  “▶ From here” plays from one bar before (songs from Songwriting: from the start of the part), “■” stops,
  “⇹ Spread” spaces the words of the bar evenly on 16ths (useful when detection put several words on one spot).
- Precise placing (bar widened): song files show a green voice curve – hills are sung syllables, marks at the top are
  syllable starts. Snap: “♪ Voice” (default, jumps to the nearest syllable start), “Grid” (16ths) or “free”.
  “♪ To voice” moves all words of the bar to their syllables (automatic detection now does this too).
  Tap a word = select and hear it, tap again = change it, hold = hear it. “⟲ Loop” repeats the bar (one beat run-up),
  also at 75 % / 50 % without changing the pitch. “◀ 10 ms / 10 ms ▶” nudges the selected word.
  “● Tap this bar”: the bar loops, tap the big button (or space bar) at every word.
- Songs with lyrics (Looper, bottom; “Ideas” and “Songs with lyrics” fold open with ▸): saves the whole song file
  together with the edited lyrics, also via “💾 Save song” in Improvisation. “Load” puts it on the next free track with
  its lyrics; “Lyrics” shares the plain text as a file. Lyrics are also saved automatically with every change.

7. CAGED
--------
- Five shapes (C, A, G, E, D) for major, minor, 7, m7 and maj7 chords.
- Views: chord shape, arpeggio, triads (on any three strings, all inversions) and whole scale.
- “Position” picks the shape that fits a four-fret area; notes shared with the next chord are ringed.
- Follows the loop; the table “In the loop” shows for every chord which shape and triad lie in your position.
- Practice: shape walk (next shape every bar) and triad ladder (one triad per beat).


8. CIRCLE OF FIFTHS
-------------------
- Major outside, relative minor in the middle, diminished inside. The seven chords of the key are
  highlighted. It jumps to the detected key and lights up the chord that is playing.
- Tap a chord to hear it; send the key to the fretboard.


9. BACKING TRACK
----------------
- Type a chord progression (e.g. “Am F C G”, “Am:2” = two bars) or choose a preset.
- Drums and bass follow the chords; add a synth sound (pads, strings, organ, keys, bell, rhythmic).
- The bar timeline shows every chord; tap to select, change or remove it.
- Tempo trainer: starts at the start tempo and gets faster every few loops up to the target.


10. SONGWRITING
---------------
- Build songs from looper parts: “→ Song” in the looper, then arrange the parts with repeats.
- Play the whole song with recordings, drums, bass and pad. Change chords, transpose, save versions.
- “Check song” gives tips on length, chorus, bridge, chords and lyrics. Variants can be auditioned
  and accepted as a new version.
- Vocals: choose your voice type or sing your lowest and highest note; the app suggests a key
  and shows target notes per chord.
- Lyrics per part and pass, with syllable count and rhyme letters. Write [Am] right before a syllable
  to place a chord change. “Lead sheet (PDF)” prints chords over the lyrics.
- “Continue with Claude”: choose a task, copy the song code, paste it into a chat with Claude,
  then paste the answer back and “Read in”. You listen first and accept it as a new version.
  The song code uses German keywords (Titel, Tonart, Akkorde …) – leave them as they are.
- “Export for Logic”: the whole song as a ZIP with recordings, MIDI (tempo, sections, chords, bass,
  drums, lyrics), lead sheet, lyrics and song code, plus a READ ME.


11. TRAINING AND TECHNIQUE
--------------------------
- Training: daily practice with timers, ear training (intervals, chord types, degrees),
  a lick library plus your own licks, and a solo analysis of a recorded solo against the chords.
- Technique: fretboard with 3-notes-per-string scales and exercise patterns, metronome,
  accompaniment (drone, drums, bass), song player and practice log.


12. BACKUP, PERFORMANCE, TROUBLESHOOTING
----------------------------------------
- “Back up everything” (Looper → App · Backup) saves ideas, patterns, practice log and settings into
  one file. Keep it in Files or iCloud; “Load backup” restores it. Do this before deleting the app.
- After a crash the last session can be restored from the bar at the top of the looper.
- “Performance ▾” shows CPU load, frame rate, dropouts, audio stalls, memory and latency.
  It can be pinned, moved and made compact.
- Playback stutters with a USB amp: choose “Stable – large audio buffer” under Performance and restart
  the app, or switch off the drum “Room”.
- Input does not open: close other audio apps (Logic, GarageBand), check the cable, tap “Open input” again.
- “Live” switches off analysis and saving while you perform; everything is caught up afterwards.
